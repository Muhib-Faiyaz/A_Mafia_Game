A guide for the living

How to play

Someone in this town is Mafia. Keep your identity secret, watch what people say, and choose whom to trust.

Your first game

  1. Enter an alias to create a room, or join with a friend's four-letter code. Each player joins on their own phone.
  2. The host chooses 1–3 Mafia and whether to include the Doctor and Detective. Start with 3–20 players; Mafia must be fewer than half. Special roles are added when there is room.
  3. Try it solo by adding bots in the lobby. When everyone is ready, the host starts the night.
  4. Tap your secret identity card to reveal your role, then tap again to hide it. This guide describes the roles without revealing anyone's identity.

Meet the roles

♠ Mafia
Choose a non-Mafia player to kill at night. Work together in your extra private Mafia chat while keeping up appearances in town chat. Win when living Mafia match or outnumber the rest of the town.
◇ Villager
You have no night action. Listen during discussion and vote to uncover the Mafia. The town wins when every Mafia player is eliminated.
+ Doctor
Protect one living player each night, including yourself. If you protect the Mafia's target, that player survives. The rescue is announced, but your identity stays secret. You are on the town's team.
⌕ Detective
Investigate one other living player each night. Your private case file tells you at dawn whether they are Mafia. Use that knowledge to help the town without making yourself an easy target.

Night, discussion, vote

  1. Mafia turn: tap a target, then confirm, or vote in Mafia chat. Matching choices kill that target; split choices select a random person from the nominated targets. With no Mafia votes, nobody is targeted. This turn ends when all living Mafia have voted or time runs out; the host cannot skip it.
  2. Doctor and Detective turns: each role chooses their action when prompted. Everyone else waits. The night result and any private investigation notes arrive at dawn.
  3. Discussion: use town chat to share clues, ask questions, and defend yourself. Voting opens when the timer ends or the host opens it.
  4. Voting: tap another living player and confirm, type /vote name in town chat, or choose /skip. The player with the most votes is eliminated. A tie, a winning skip vote, or no votes means nobody is eliminated.
  5. The next night begins after votes are counted. Continue until the town or Mafia wins. Eliminated players can watch but cannot act or send messages during play.

Session points

Points appear under your name after the game ends and stay with you across games in the same room.

  • Each Mafia player gets 1 point for a Mafia team win.
  • Each surviving town player gets 1 point for a town win.
  • The Doctor gets 1 point for each successful rescue.
  • The Detective gets 1 point for each different Mafia player correctly identified.
